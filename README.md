# You Click the Bomb You Lose!

A small game I made in p5.js. There are six boxes and some of them have bombs hidden in them. Pick a safe box to get a point. Pick a bomb and it's game over.

Play it here: https://aurangzeb002.github.io/bomb-click-game/

## How it works

- Click a box. If it's safe your score goes up and the bombs move to new boxes
- At 5 points there are 2 bombs, and at 10 points there are 3
- If you hit a bomb the screen flashes red and a game over message comes up
- Click anywhere to play again

## Made with

JavaScript, p5.js, HTML and CSS

## How to run

Download or clone the repo and open index.html in your browser.
